## Observation

All three AI models correctly classified the 10 customer support messages using both Zero-Shot and Few-Shot prompting, resulting in 100% accuracy in each case. Although the accuracy remained the same, the Few-Shot prompts produced more structured and consistent outputs. For example, Claude and Gemini followed the requested formatting more closely when examples were provided. This shows that while both prompting techniques can perform equally well on simple classification tasks, Few-Shot prompting helps improve response consistency and reduces ambiguity in the output format.
